Who We Are

{% if homepage and homepage.who_we_are_text %} {{ homepage.who_we_are_text }} {% else %} Mission for Meaningful Change (MMC) is a Somali humanitarian and development organisation building on 17 years of institutional experience. We work with crisis-affected communities to meet urgent needs, restore livelihoods, strengthen resilience and local systems, and expand pathways to self-reliance and inclusive prosperity. By combining frontline response, technical expertise, evidence-based advocacy and social enterprise, we connect humanitarian action with sustainable development and meaningful change. {% endif %}
